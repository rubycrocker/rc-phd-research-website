Temporal Analysis of Emotion Perception in Film Music

Investigating how emotional meaning develops through time in film music using audience studies, computational analysis, semantic emotion modelling, cinema experiments, and composer interviews.

Explore the Research

Research Overview

Film music plays an important role in shaping how audiences experience stories. This research explores how emotional meaning emerges through music over time, how listeners describe those experiences, and how emotional change can be studied computationally, psychologically, creatively, and empirically.

Current Research Areas

FME-24 Dataset

A film music emotion dataset containing temporal emotion annotations, valence-arousal ratings, natural-language descriptions, acoustic features, and participant metadata.

Explore Dataset

FMSER Framework

Film Music Semantic Emotion Regions (FMSER) provides a semantic representation of emotional meaning derived directly from listener descriptions.

EMORTION Studies

Cinema experiments investigating emotional responses, immersion, sound design, music, audio features and audience experiences.
